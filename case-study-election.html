Overview

What this project is

This project analyzes the India General Election Results 2024 using Power BI, providing interactive dashboards to explore election performance across alliances, states, constituencies, political parties, and candidates. The dashboard suite enables users to explore election trends through dynamic visualizations, drill-through reports, maps, KPIs, and filtering.

Business Problem

Why it matters

Election results data is large, granular, and scattered across constituency, party, and candidate levels — hard to interpret at a glance in raw form. Political analysts, journalists, and researchers need a way to move fluidly between the national picture and constituency-level detail without re-running manual analysis for every question.

Objective

What the dashboard needed to deliver

Design an interactive Power BI solution providing a national election overview, alliance-wise seat distribution, state-level political analysis, constituency-wise election insights, and candidate performance comparison — packaged as six fully interactive dashboards for political analysts, researchers, and the general public.

Dataset

What the analysis is built on

Structured election data across three source tables: overall election results, party-level results, and constituency-level data — covering winners, runners-up, vote share, postal and EVM votes, margins, and party/alliance affiliation.

Methodology

How it was approached

Raw election data was cleaned in Excel, transformed through Power Query ETL, and modeled in Power BI with DAX measures driving the KPIs. The final solution is split into six purpose-built dashboards:

01

Overview Analysis

National KPIs — NDA, I.N.D.I.A, and independent seat counts, alliance comparison

02

State Demographic Analysis

State-wise map, majority alliance, constituency bubble map, drill-through

03

Political Landscape by State

State filter, party seat share, donut chart, alliance comparison

04

Constituency Analysis

Winner, runner-up, vote share, postal/EVM votes, margin of victory

05

Details Grid

Full interactive data table with export to Excel

06

Landing Page

Dashboard navigation with interactive cards and bookmarks

Key Features

What the dashboard supports

  • Interactive filters and cross-filtering across all six dashboards
  • Drill-through reports from national to constituency level
  • Dynamic KPIs and bookmark-based navigation
  • Map visualizations for state and constituency-level results
  • Full data export to Excel from the details grid
Business Value

Who this is built for

Designed to help political analysts, election researchers, journalists, government agencies, students, and the general public understand election outcomes through interactive visual analytics rather than static reports.
