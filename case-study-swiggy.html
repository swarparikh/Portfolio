Overview

What this project is

This project is an interactive Excel dashboard built to analyze Swiggy's food delivery sales performance across India. Using Power Query, Power Pivot, and PivotTables/PivotCharts, sales, ratings, and order data are brought together into a single executive view — helping stakeholders track revenue trends, customer satisfaction, and regional performance at a glance, entirely within Excel.

Business Problem

Why it matters

Raw Swiggy order-level data on its own doesn't answer the questions a business actually asks: which cities and states are driving revenue, whether veg or non-veg orders dominate sales, how performance shifts week to week and quarter to quarter, and where customer satisfaction stands. A Business Requirements Document (BRD) was used to define exactly which KPIs and charts stakeholders needed before any dashboard work began.

Requirements (BRD)

KPIs and charts defined upfront

KPI's

  • Total Sales (₹) — overall revenue from food orders
  • Average Rating — customer satisfaction across restaurants
  • Average Order Value (₹) — revenue per order
  • Ratings Count — total customer reviews
  • Total Orders — number of food orders received

Chart's

  • Monthly Sales Trend
  • Daily Sales Trend
  • Total Sales by Food Type (Veg vs Non-Veg)
  • Total Sales by State (map visualization)
  • Quarterly Performance Summary
  • Top 5 Cities by Sales
  • Weekly Trend Analysis
BRD — KPI requirements gathered before dashboard design
Dataset

What the analysis is built on

Swiggy order-level sales data covering restaurant name, food category (Desi Ghee, Chinese, Pasta, Roll, Sides, and more), state, city, order date, rating, and order value — cleaned and modeled entirely inside Excel using Power Query and Power Pivot.

Methodology

How it was approached

Process

  • Raw dataset ingestion
  • Data cleaning via Power Query
  • Data modeling via Power Pivot
  • DAX measures for KPI cards
  • PivotTables & PivotCharts
  • Executive dashboard design

Analysis performed

  • Sales trend analysis — monthly, daily, weekly
  • Food type split — Veg vs Non-Veg revenue share
  • Geographic analysis — state and city-level sales
  • Quarterly performance — Sales, Rating, Orders combined
  • Restaurant and category-level filtering via slicers
Dashboard

The final result

KPI cards, slicers for Month/Category/Restaurant, and seven interactive charts combined into a single executive dashboard view.

Key Insights

What came out of it

  • Total Sales reached ₹53.01M across 197.43K total orders, with an average rating of 4.34
  • Average order value stood at ₹268.51, backed by 5.59M customer ratings
  • Non-Veg orders made up 63% of total sales vs 37% for Veg
  • Bengaluru led all cities with ₹5.46M in sales, followed by Lucknow, Hyderabad, Mumbai, and New Delhi
  • Q2 recorded the highest quarterly sales at ₹19.9M, slightly ahead of Q1's ₹19.7M
  • Weekly sales trends show consistent performance with periodic peaks, useful for identifying high-demand periods
